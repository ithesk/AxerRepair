Repair workshop management

Device intake, customer tracking, notifications and quotation signing

Extends Odoo's Repair app with what a workshop serving end customers actually needs: phones, tablets, watches and laptops that come in, get diagnosed, repaired and handed back.

Every device is booked in with its IMEI or serial number, its fault and the condition it arrived in. The customer gets a link to follow the repair at any time, and a message at each change of state.

What you get

Device intake

IMEI or serial number, unlock code, device type and fifteen checks on the condition of the device at intake: screen, touch, cameras, signal, wifi, microphone, charging, buttons and battery. Whatever could not be tested is recorded as such.

Customer tracking

A page of their own with the history of their repair, protected by a link with a token. No account, no password: just their link.

Automatic notifications

A message to the customer at every change of state through a configurable WhatsApp gateway. Optionally drafted by an AI assistant.

Quotation signing

Send the quotation for the customer to sign, and record the acceptance on the repair itself.

Receipt and labels

Intake sheet with barcode and tracking QR, plus device labels. Sent to CUPS if a print server is configured; downloaded as PDF if not.

Branches and transfers

Each repair belongs to a branch, with its own printers. Devices move between branches with confirmed reception.

Bulk intake

Book in several devices from the same customer by scanning the IMEIs, one by one or by pasting the whole list.

Analysis

Pivot table and charts over the repairs, arranged by each user as they like: by technician, device type, fault, IMEI, customer, branch or how the customer heard about you. With filters for the day to day, such as overdue and due today. The result can be saved as a favourite and exported to a spreadsheet.

Integrations, all optional

WhatsApp, digital signature, IMEI lookup, AI assistant and synchronisation with a second Odoo database. Each has its own switch under Settings → Repair Workshop and, while it is off, the module calls no external service. The name and logo on receipts and labels come from your company record.

A look at the module

Every repair at a glance. Overdue jobs show in red; each user picks their own columns from the twenty-seven available.

The condition the device arrived in. Fifteen checks recorded on intake, so nothing is disputed on handover.

Analysis you arrange yourself. Drag technician, device type, fault or branch into rows and columns and pick what to measure.

Several devices at once. Scan the IMEI of each one, or paste the whole list.

Every integration has its switch. While it is off, the module calls no external service at all.
